abtem.ebsd.reference#

Reference patterns: EBSD intensity over the whole hemisphere.

A reciprocity calculation is accurate for directions near its slab’s axis, so EBSDReferencePattern tiles the hemisphere into patches, calculates each in its own slab, and joins the results in the crystal frame.

API#

class abtem.ebsd.reference.EBSDReferencePattern(...)[source]#

Bases: abtem.core.utils.CopyMixin, abtem.core.utils.EqualityMixin

A hemisphere-wide EBSD reference pattern, tiled from zone-axis patches.

Parameters:

  • atoms (ase.Atoms or FrozenPhonons) – The unit cell of the crystal. Give FrozenPhonons of it for thermal vibrations: every slab is displaced as the unit cell would be, and the pattern is averaged over the configurations, at num_configs times the cost.

  • energy (float) – Energy of the incident beam [eV].

  • slab_cell (tuple of three float) –

    Dimensions of the slab cut for each patch [Å]; the third entry is the thickness along the slab’s axis. There is no default, since the right size depends on the crystal and the accuracy needed.

    The bands build up over the first ~100 Å, so 60-100 Å is a typical thickness. The width controls the seams: a wave at an angle θ to the slab’s axis moves θ T sideways over a thickness T and then meets the slab’s non-periodic edge. 30-40 Å wide slabs keep this error to a few percent. To make a run cheaper, lower gpts rather than the slab size.

  • n_patches (int, optional) – Number of zone-axis patches tiling each hemisphere (default 400). More patches keep every direction closer to its slab’s axis.

  • gpts (int, optional) – Pixels along each axis of a projected image (default 128). Sets the default density of the sampled directions.

  • projection (str or HemisphereProjection, optional) – Projection whose even grid the directions are drawn from, 'lambert' (default) or 'stereographic'. The equal-area Lambert grid samples the hemisphere uniformly and can be interpolated to either projection.

  • sampling (float, optional) – Real-space sampling of the potentials [Å]. Defaults to potential_sampling() for atoms.

  • slice_thickness (float, optional) – Multislice slice thickness [Å] (default 1.0).

  • direction_gpts (int, optional) – Number of points per axis of the grid the directions are drawn from. Defaults to ceil(1.15 * gpts), made odd, so that a Lambert grid is written to an EMsoft file as calculated.

  • max_angle (float, optional) – Angular radius of each patch [mrad]; directions farther than this from their patch’s axis are left out, with a warning. Defaults to patch_half_angle(), which covers every direction.

  • repetitions (tuple of three int, optional) – Repetitions of atoms in the block the slabs are cut from; see rotated_slab().

  • origin (np.ndarray, optional) – Point of atoms to place at the centre of every slab, passed to rotated_slab(). Defaults to the centroid.

  • hemisphere ({'north', 'south', 'both'}, optional) – Which directions to calculate (default ‘north’). For a centrosymmetric crystal the other hemisphere is the inversion image, which the pattern provides (SphericalPattern.southern). For a crystal without an inversion centre, such as GaN, calculate ‘both’. The southern directions are (x, y, -z) below the northern ones, as in EMsoft.

  • centrosymmetric (bool, optional) – Whether the crystal has an inversion centre. Recorded in the pattern; it decides whether the inversion image stands in for the hemisphere not calculated without a warning. If None (default), it is determined with spglib when first needed, which can be slow or fail for a large cell without lattice symmetry. Without spglib (the abtem[ebsd] extra) the pattern records it as unknown.

  • backscatter_energy (float or sequence of float or BaseDistribution, optional) – Energies of the backscattered electrons [eV]; several add a leading BackscatterEnergyAxis, or are averaged over when a distribution asks for it. See EBSD.

  • depth_weight – Passed to EBSD.

  • device – Passed to EBSD.

Notes

Every atom emits by its backscattering cross-section and is lit evenly, as in EMsoft (see abtem.ebsd.emission).

A direction d is where the backscattered electrons go. By reciprocity it is calculated with a plane wave travelling along -d, so each patch is calculated in a slab cut along the opposite of its centre.

The intensity leaving a crystal along -d equals the intensity leaving its inversion image along d. The southern hemisphere is therefore calculated as the northern hemisphere of the crystal inverted about the slab origin. For a crystal with an inversion centre at the origin the two calculations are identical.

abtem.ebsd.reference.patch_half_angle(...) → float[source]#

Angular radius of each patch needed to tile the hemisphere.

Each of n_patches points of a Fibonacci lattice has a cell of solid angle \(2\pi / n\); the radius is that of a disc through the corners of a regular hexagon of that area, enlarged by coverage.

Parameters:

  • n_patches (int) – Number of patches tiling the hemisphere.

  • coverage (float, optional) – Factor by which the covering radius is enlarged (default 1.2).

Returns:

half_angle – Angular radius of one patch [mrad].

Return type:

float
